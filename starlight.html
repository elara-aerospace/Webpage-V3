Starlight.

A reusable suborbital rocket for research beyond the atmosphere. Designed to carry experiments into high-speed flight and reduced gravity.

Explore the vehicle ↓
Research flight. Reduced gravity. Repeat.Vehicle overview ↓

The vehicle

More room
for research.

Starlight is being developed for atmospheric science, microgravity research, and technology demonstrations. Electric pumps power ascent; a two-stage parachute system recovers the vehicle and payload.

The figures shown are development targets, rather than demonstrated flight performance.

Design targets
ApogeeUp to 150 km / 93 mi
Launch mass~1,350 kg / 2,976 lb
Payload capacityUp to 100 kg / 220 lb
Flight duration10 min
Burn duration80 sec
Maximum speedMach 5
Engine thrust28 kN / 6,294 lbf
